# The DTC Commercial Scorecard
### 8 things that tell you if your product video is costing you sales

**How this works:** Pull up your most recent product video — the one on your Instagram, 
your product page, or your ads. Then answer each question honestly.
Score at the bottom.

---

## THE FIRST IMPRESSION (first 3 seconds)

**1. In the first 3 seconds, does something MOVE or CHANGE that makes you want to keep watching?**
☐ Yes  ☐ No
If the first frame is a static product shot, most people scroll past before the video even starts.

**2. Does your video look like it was PRODUCED — good lighting, sharp image, intentional framing?**
☐ Yes  ☐ No
Phone footage with room lighting reads as "small brand." Buyers feel the difference, even if they can't name it.

---

## THE STORY

**3. Does your video show the product being USED by a person, not just sitting on a surface?**
☐ Yes  ☐ No
Product-on-desk shots don't sell. Someone applying the serum, wearing the watch, unboxing the package — that sells.

**4. If you watched with the sound OFF, would you still understand what the product is and why you should care?**
☐ Yes  ☐ No
85% of Instagram video is watched on mute. If your video needs audio to make sense, you've lost 85% of viewers.

---

## THE QUALITY

**5. Are your cuts paced well — no single shot lingers too long?**
☐ Yes  ☐ No
Shots that hang for 4+ seconds without anything happening lose attention. Commercial pacing is faster than you think.

**6. Does your video have a clear END — a logo, a tagline, a call to action — not just a fade out?**
☐ Yes  ☐ No
A video that trails off feels unfinished. The last frame should seal the brand in their head.

---

## THE RESULT

**7. Have you ever had someone comment or DM you specifically about your video — "nice production" or "who shot this?"**
☐ Yes  ☐ No
If nobody ever mentions your video quality, it's invisible. Good work gets noticed.

**8. Is your current video converting? (Are people buying after watching, or are you getting "nice product" comments with no sales?)**
☐ Yes  ☐ No
The only metric that matters. A pretty video that doesn't sell is just expensive art.

---

## YOUR SCORE

Count your NO answers. Each NO is a problem.

**0-2 NOs:** Your video is solid. Small tweaks might help, but you're not bleeding.

**3-5 NOs:** Your video has real gaps. People are scrolling past or landing and leaving. You're losing money.

**6-8 NOs:** Your video is actively hurting your brand. Every rupee you spend on ads right now is being wasted sending people to a video that doesn't convert.
