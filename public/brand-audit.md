# The 5-Minute Brand Audit
### 12 things that tell you if your website is costing you sales

**How this works:** Go to your website. Answer each question honestly. 
If you answer YES to 4 or more, your brand is bleeding conversions right now.
Every YES is a silent reason someone left without buying.

---

## FIRST IMPRESSION (the 50-millisecond test)

**1. Does your site load in under 3 seconds?**
☐ Yes  ☐ No
If no: 53% of mobile visitors leave if it takes longer than 3 seconds.

**2. In the first 2 seconds, does a stranger know exactly what you sell?**
☐ Yes  ☐ No
If no: they won't scroll to find out. They'll leave.

**3. Does your logo look like it was made by a professional, not Fiverr?**
☐ Yes  ☐ No
Your logo is the first thing they see. If it looks cheap, your product looks cheap.

---

## TYPOGRAPHY (yes, this matters)

**4. Are you using more than 2 fonts on your site?**
☐ Yes  ☐ No
YES is the WRONG answer here. One font for headlines, one for body. More than that reads as messy.

**5. Is your body text at least 16px?**
☐ Yes  ☐ No
Anything smaller and your visitors are squinting. On mobile, it's worse.

**6. Is your line spacing comfortable (not crammed together)?**
☐ Yes  ☐ No
Tight lines are hard to read. Hard to read = they stop reading.

---

## COLORS THAT CONVERT

**7. Is your "Buy Now" or "Add to Cart" button a color that stands out from everything else on the page?**
☐ Yes  ☐ No
If your CTA blends in, people don't click. It should be the one color that breaks the palette.

**8. Do you have more than 4 colors in your palette (not counting product photos)?**
☐ Yes  ☐ No
YES is bad. A tight palette (2-3 core + 1 accent) reads as intentional. A rainbow reads as messy.

---

## TRUST SIGNALS

**9. If a customer lands on your product page, can they find a real review or testimonial within 5 seconds?**
☐ Yes  ☐ No
No reviews visible = no social proof. No social proof = no trust.

**10. Does your site show real product photos you took, not just supplier images?**
☐ Yes  ☐ No
Supplier images make you look like a dropshipper. Real photos make you look like a brand.

---

## THE CLOSE

**11. On mobile, can someone buy your product in 3 taps or less from the homepage?**
☐ Yes  ☐ No
Mobile is 70%+ of your traffic. If buying takes work, they won't.

**12. After someone buys, do they land on a page that makes them feel good about their purchase?**
☐ Yes  ☐ No
The confirmation page is the last thing they see. Make it feel like they made the right decision.

---

## YOUR SCORE

Count your YES answers (treat questions 4 and 8 as "yes" if you answered NO — those were reversed).

**0-3 YES:** Your brand is solid. Small tweaks might help, but you're not bleeding.

**4-7 YES:** You have real conversion problems. Every day you don't fix them, you're losing sales.

**8-12 YES:** Your brand is actively hurting your business. Fix this before you spend another rupee on ads.
